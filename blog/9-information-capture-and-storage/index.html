How much do you know about your memory?

Information Capture and Storage

Psychologists say that for each piece of new information to be assimilated 
and stored in the memory it has to be repeated minimum seven times. 
This means that if we want to learn foreign words, technical terms and definitions, 
we need to repeat them several times, which requires time input.

Free Time

Where can we get additional time for learning? 
We can make a better use of time otherwise wasted in queues, in transport, 
or we can study while relaxing on the sofa.

We can get this time with the help of our mobile phones.

Guessing Made Easy!

You can read the definition of the word, which is hidden behind asterisks.
To guess the word, you have to enter letter by letter, choosing one of the three suggested letters. 
This makes the guessing process easy. You can get hints. 
The hints options are: you can either open any of the letters in the word, 
or you can press "Hint" . This will help you guess the right answer.
